What It Costs

Most whole-house cleanouts in Austin run $1,000 to $3,000 or more with a junk company, and that is just to haul it away. Ours works differently: we put a value on what is inside and count it against the price. That lands in one of three places.

Most homes

You pay us

Some homes

It is free

When what is inside covers our time, the cleanout costs you nothing. We tell you that from the photos and the walkthrough, not after.

A few homes

We pay you

When there is more value inside than it costs us to clear it, we make you an offer for the contents and clear the house for free.

Whole homes: typically $2,500 – $4,500 for 2 to 3 days, everything included. A few rooms plus the garage: from $549. The number we text you is the number, unless the house changes before we get there.

A Recent Job

A three-bedroom house in Southwest Austin.

Mom had moved to assisted living. The house needed to become a rental to help pay for her care, and her daughter had been trying to face it alone. Fifty boxes of papers nobody had opened, a piano, a doll collection, china in every cabinet, and a garage that had not been walked through in a decade.

We worked in day-long blocks. She sat at a table while the crew brought her boxes to decide on. The antiques she wanted sold were set aside. Anything personal found along the way went into one container that came back to her at the end of the day. The rest went out load by load, and the house was left swept and ready for the next step.
