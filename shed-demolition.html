What We Don't Do

We do light demolition, and we are upfront about where that ends. This keeps you, your house and our crew safe.

  • No structural work. Nothing attached to your house, and nothing load-bearing.
  • No permits. We do not pull permits. If you are not sure whether you need one, check with your city or HOA first.
  • No electrical or plumbing disconnects. If your shed has power, have a licensed electrician disconnect it before we come. Same for any water lines.
  • Concrete slabs, case by case. We do not promise slab removal. Ask us and we will tell you honestly from the photos.
  • No hazardous materials. No paint, chemicals, propane tanks or anything hazardous. Tell us what is in there and we will point you to the right drop-off.
