What We Take

If two people can carry it and it fits on a trailer, we take it. Here is what we haul most weeks.

Mattresses & furniture

Mattresses, box springs, couches, sectionals, recliners, dressers, desks, tables, bed frames. Leave it in the garage or driveway and we will grab it.

Appliances

Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, water heaters, stoves. Working ones get rehomed when we can.

Garages, sheds & storage units

The whole thing, or just what you point at. We sort as we go, set aside anything you want to keep, and sweep before we leave.

Hot tubs & shed teardowns

We cut it up, haul it off, and leave the pad clean. Text a photo with something for scale and we will price it the same day.

Move-out & rental turnover junk

Whatever the last tenant or the move left behind. Landlords and agents: we can be there between showings.

Yard & storm debris

Fence panels, branches, old pavers, playscapes, trampolines, the pile that has been sitting by the fence since spring.

We do not take paint, chemicals, propane tanks, tires or anything hazardous. Tell us what is there and we will point you to the right drop-off.
