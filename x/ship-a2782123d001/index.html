Coin shipping for today

Thank you for doing this! 10 silver coins, 5 envelopes, 1 trip to the post office.

These need to go out today. Four of the five are due today. Hand them to a person at the post office counter. Don't use a blue box or a drop slot.

1. Grab everything

2. Match each coin to its envelope

Use the cards below. They're all silver dollars, so go by the year, on the side with the walking lady, near the bottom. Tap any photo to zoom in.

There are two 2003 coins (Robert and Steve) and two 2022 coins (Tommy and Randy). For those, compare spots, scratches and color to the photos. If they look the same, make your best guess and tell Jordan which ones.

3. Pack each coin

  1. Put one coin in one Ziploc, push out the air and seal it.
  2. Fold the bag around the coin into a small, flat package.
  3. Wrap it in bubble wrap once or twice and tape it closed.
  4. Put it in its envelope, snug enough that it can't slide around. Steve gets 4 coins, Tommy gets 2 and Debbie gets 2, each one bagged and wrapped by itself.
  5. Seal the envelope and tape over the flap. If the top left corner is blank, write our home address there as the return address.

5. At the post office

No postage is bought yet, so the clerk will weigh each one and you pay at the counter. Expect roughly $50 to $60 total on a card. You can tell them:

"This one for Robert Wheeler goes Priority Mail, in a Flat Rate Envelope if that's cheapest. These other four go Ground Advantage. They all need tracking, please."

Robert's envelope: if the clerk puts it in a free Priority Flat Rate Envelope, copy his address over from the one Jordan wrote, or slide Jordan's envelope inside if it closes normally.

Skip any extra insurance if they offer it. USPS barely covers silver coins unless they go Registered Mail, so it isn't worth paying for.

Before you leave: keep every receipt and make sure you can tell which tracking number goes with which buyer. Writing the buyer's name next to each number works great.

6. When you're done

Take a photo of the receipts showing all 5 tracking numbers and text it to Jordan. Those numbers get added to eBay, which marks the orders shipped and tells the buyers.
